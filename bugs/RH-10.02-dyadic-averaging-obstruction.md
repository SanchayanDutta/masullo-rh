# RH-10.02 A uniform dyadic averaging strategy is obstructed

[Repository index](../README.md) | [Editing rules and source edition](../README.md#updating-entries)

**Kind** Obstruction to a proposed strategy. **Repair state** Local verified. **Patch state** Not recorded.

**Source** [Independent bounds for the Möbius cutoff in Masullo’s criterion](../repairs/independent_masullo_progress.pdf#page=1), dated November 21, 2026, §1, averaging obstruction, p. 1.

## Finding

For any fixed $0<\delta<1$, the estimate

$$
\frac1N\sum_{N\le n<2N}H_n\ll N^{1-\delta},
\qquad N=2^j,
$$

cannot hold on every sufficiently large dyadic block. This is an obstruction discussed in the independent report. It is not identified here as a claim printed in Masullo's manuscript.

The balance estimate in [RH-10.01](../repairs/RH-10.01-balance-and-residual-energy.md) would imply

$$
\sum_{N\le n<2N}|m(n)|^2\ll N^{-\delta}.
$$

Cauchy–Schwarz on dyadic blocks would then make

$$
\int_1^\infty m(\lfloor t\rfloor)t^{-s-1}\,dt
$$

absolutely convergent and holomorphic on $\Re s>-(1+\delta)/2$. On $\Re s>0$ its value is $1/[s\zeta(1+s)]$. A known critical-line zero $\rho$ gives a pole at $s=\rho-1$, inside the larger domain, contradicting the asserted holomorphy.

## Proposed change of strategy

Seek favorable individual cutoffs or a sparse unbounded sequence. The obstruction concerns this particular uniform power-saving average and does not exclude such selections. See [RH-10.06](RH-10.06-cofinal-cutoff-selection.md).
