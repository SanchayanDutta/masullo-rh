# RH-10.06 The cofinal arithmetic upper bound remains open

[Repository index](../README.md) | [Editing rules and source edition](../README.md#updating-entries)

**Kind** Open arithmetic obligation. **Repair state** Open. **Patch state** Not recorded.

**Source** [Independent bounds for the Möbius cutoff in Masullo’s criterion](../repairs/independent_masullo_progress.pdf#page=5), dated November 21, 2026, §4, p. 5.

## Required result

With the notation of [RH-10.03](../repairs/RH-10.03-local-mertens-localization.md), establish integers $X_j\to\infty$ with

$$
S_{X_j}\le X_j^{2+o(1)}.
$$

Equivalently for this notation, the bound must hold eventually along the selected sequence with exponent $2+\varepsilon$ for every fixed $\varepsilon>0$. The independent report does not establish such a sequence. Its level-3 and level-4 witnesses and its bounded-range comparison do not provide one.

## Exact correlation expansion

The report writes

$$
S_X=\sum_{X<m,n<Q}\mu(m)\mu(n)\bigl(Q-\max(m,n)\bigr).
$$

The diagonal is at most $Q^2$. Pairs with $1\le|m-n|\le H$ contribute at most $2HQ^2$ in absolute value. With $H=X^{o(1)}$ and $Q=64X\lceil\log X\rceil$, both already have the allowed exponent $2+o(1)$.

The remaining task is a suitable upper bound on the signed contribution of the distant pairs on an unbounded sequence. Termwise absolute values yield only $O(Q^3)$ and do not meet the target. A two-sided absolute bound on the signed total would be stronger than necessary.
