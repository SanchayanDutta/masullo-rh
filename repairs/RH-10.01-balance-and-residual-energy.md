# RH-10.01 Balance follows from cutoff energy

## Finding

The report removes a separate balance restriction from one sufficient asymptotic test. This does not remove the balance condition from the original finite-level certificate test.

For an integer $X\ge2$, put

$$
m(X)=\sum_{n\le X}\frac{\mu(n)}n,\qquad b=Xm(X),\qquad
C_X(t)=\sum_{n\le X}\mu(n)\left\lfloor\frac tn\right\rfloor,
$$

$$
H_X=\sum_{k=X}^{X^2-1}\frac{C_X(k)^2}{k(k+1)}.
$$

The fractional-part sum $A_X(t)=\sum_{n\le X}\mu(n)\{t/n\}$ satisfies $tm(X)=C_X(t)+A_X(t)$ and $|A_X(t)|\le X$. Comparing norms against $dt/t^2$ on $[X^2/2,X^2)$ gives

$$
|b|\le\sqrt2(\sqrt{H_X}+1).
$$

For the primitive residual energy $E_X$ defined in the cutoff note referenced by the report, the displayed estimates yield

$$
\sqrt{E_X}\le6(1+\sqrt{H_X}),\qquad E_X\le72(1+H_X).
$$

Thus a cofinal sequence with $1+H_X\le X^{o(1)}$ suffices through the referenced zero barrier without a separate $|b|\le1$ premise. The cutoff note and its condition (A) are referenced but not included in the new PDF. The implication from this energy bound to its original zero barrier remains attributed to the report.
