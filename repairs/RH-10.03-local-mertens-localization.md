# RH-10.03 Local Mertens localization at unrestricted cutoffs

[Repository index](../README.md) | [Editing rules and source edition](../README.md#updating-entries)

**Kind** Independent analytic progress. **Repair state** Conditional. **Patch state** Not recorded.

**Source** [Independent bounds for the Möbius cutoff in Masullo’s criterion](independent_masullo_progress.pdf#page=1), dated November 21, 2026, §2, equations (1)–(3), pp. 1–4.

## Statement

For an integer $X\ge2$, use natural logarithms and set

$$
r=\lceil\log X\rceil,\qquad T=8rX,\qquad Q=8T=64rX,
$$

$$
M(t)=\sum_{n\le t}\mu(n),\qquad
S_X=\sum_{k=X}^{Q-1}(M(k)-M(X))^2.
$$

For each fixed nontrivial zeta zero $\rho=\beta+i\gamma$ with $1/2\le\beta<1$, the report proves that there are $c_\rho>0$ and $X_0(\rho)$ such that every integer $X\ge X_0(\rho)$ satisfies

$$
S_X\ge c_\rho XT^{2\beta}
\asymp_\rho X^{1+2\beta}(\log X)^{2\beta}.
$$

There is no balance restriction. Both the constant and the threshold may depend on the zero. Möbius values are needed only through $Q=64X\lceil\log X\rceil$ for this finite statistic.

## Proof structure and consequence

The report uses the fractional-part Mellin identity, an $r$-fold box smoothing, and a three-scale cutoff with two vanishing moments. The coefficient polynomial uses $z=2^{-\rho}$ and roots $z$ and $2z$. Its cancellations and a Fourier tail estimate give equation (2), with sign $-1/\rho$. The transfer to $M(t)-M(X)$ gives equation (3), with sign $+1/\rho$. Unweighted Cauchy–Schwarz yields the stated lower bound.

A cofinal sequence with $S_X\le X^{2+o(1)}$ would rule out every $\beta>1/2$ and imply ordinary RH by reflection. A single fixed critical-line zero forces $S_X\gg X^2\log X$ for every sufficiently large cutoff, consistent with the requested upper exponent.

## Connection with the divisor-sum energy

The report also connects the two sufficient upper bounds on the same sequence. Let $D_X(t)=M(t)-M(X)$ and $F_X(t)=1-C_X(t)$, each extended by zero below $X$. Möbius inversion gives $D_X(t)=\sum_j\mu(j)F_X(t/j)$. In the weighted norm on $[X,Q]$ with measure $dt/t^2$, a dilation costs at most $j^{-1/2}$, giving

$$
\|D_X\|\le2\sqrt{Q/X}\bigl(\|C_X\|+X^{-1/2}\bigr).
$$

Eventually $Q<X^2$, so $\|C_X\|^2\le H_X$ and $S_X\le Q^2\|D_X\|^2$. Thus a cofinal sequence with $1+H_X\le X^{o(1)}$ gives $S_X\le X^{2+o(1)}$ on that same sequence. This deduction can be checked from the displayed definitions. The separate assertion that the original balanced condition (A) supplies the needed bound remains attributed to the report because that original note is not included.
