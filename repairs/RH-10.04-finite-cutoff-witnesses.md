## Original finite test

With $b=X\sum_{n\le X}\mu(n)/n$ and $H_X$ as in [RH-10.01](RH-10.01-balance-and-residual-energy.md), the reported test requires both

$$
|b|\le1,\qquad [h(1+h^2)(H_X+25)]^h<X^2.
$$

| Level | Cutoff | Reported bounds | Exact final comparison |
|---|---|---|---|
| $h=3$ | $X=25{,}295$ | $\lvert b\rvert<0.29$ and $H_X\le2{,}020{,}195/2^{24}<1$ | $780^3=474{,}552{,}000<639{,}837{,}025=X^2$ |
| $h=4$ | $X=4{,}715{,}877$ | $\lvert b\rvert\le0.021$, $M(X)=-37$, and $H_X<4$ | $1972^4=15{,}122{,}640{,}998{,}656<22{,}239{,}495{,}879{,}129=X^2$ |

## Evidence boundary

The displayed integer powers and inequalities were checked independently. An independent elementary Möbius sieve with reciprocal enclosures over $2^{80}$ also confirms both balance bounds and $M(4{,}715{,}877)=-37$.

The source-reported energy bounds and the claimed GNU C++17 verifier runs were not independently reproduced. The new PDF does not include verifier source or execution logs.

For the level-4 tail the report uses $T_0=2^{30}$, $V\le151{,}283$, and low energy at most $156{,}786{,}031/2^{32}<0.037$. Given those reported inputs, the displayed arithmetic gives

$$
\frac54V\left(\frac{X^2}{T_0^2}+\frac4{X^2}\right)<3.65,
$$

$$
H_X<0.037+(1.92+0.021+0.001)^2=3.808364<4.
$$
