## Statement

Use the same integer $X$, $T=8X\lceil\log X\rceil$, and $Q=8T$ as in [RH-10.03](RH-10.03-local-mertens-localization.md). Define the exact rational statistic

$$
L_X=\sum_{k=X}^{Q-1}\frac{|M(k)-M(X)|}{k}.
$$

We use $L_X$ here to distinguish this statistic from the function $F_X(t)$ in the report's proof. For each fixed zero $\rho=\beta+i\gamma$ with $1/2\le\beta<1$, the report gives $L_X\gg_\rho T^\beta$ for every sufficiently large integer $X$. It follows that an unbounded sequence with $L_X\le X^{1/2+o(1)}$ would imply ordinary RH.

## Relation to the second moment

On the same sequence, Cauchy–Schwarz gives

$$
L_X\le \sqrt{S_X}
\left(\sum_{k=X}^{Q-1}k^{-2}\right)^{1/2}
\ll\sqrt{S_X/X}.
$$

Thus the proposed second-moment upper bound implies the proposed first-moment upper bound. The converse is not asserted here.
