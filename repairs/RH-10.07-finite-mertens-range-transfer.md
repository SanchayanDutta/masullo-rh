## Reported input and deduction

The independent report cites Hurst, Mathematics of Computation 87 (2018), for the finite verification $|M(k)|\le\sqrt{k}$ through $10^{16}$. That external computation was not rechecked for this update.

Assuming this input, for $X\le k<Q\le10^{16}$,

$$
|M(k)-M(X)|^2
\le(\sqrt{k}+\sqrt X)^2\le4k,
$$

so

$$
S_X\le2Q(Q+1).
$$

For $Q=64X\lceil\log X\rceil$, the condition holds in particular for every integer $2\le X\le5\cdot10^{12}$. At the upper endpoint $\lceil\log X\rceil=30$ and $Q=9.6\cdot10^{15}$.
